# Tutorials: environments

Five short tutorials on how a world **looks**: the light, the colours, the mood and the screen. Each one is about ten to twenty minutes, adds one idea and ends with something you can see. Do them in order the first time.

| Tutorial | You build | New idea |
|---|---|---|
| [1. A day in the valley](/basic/world/tutorials/a-day-in-the-valley) | A warm valley with a morning mist | The Environment Debugger, the path of the sun and the *Sun Light* of an environment, fog that lies low |
| [2. Colour grading with a LUT](/basic/world/tutorials/colour-grading-with-a-lut) | A graded world, first with numbers, then with a picture you made | The colour grade and the LUT |
| [3. A scary dungeon](/basic/world/tutorials/a-scary-dungeon) | A dark dungeon that shakes and goes red at its boss door | A different mood for one world, screen looks and camera shake from events |
| [4. Can everyone see your world?](/basic/world/tutorials/can-everyone-see-your-world) | A check of your world for players with colour blindness | The colour blindness view and the player's own filter |
| [5. A mood for each zone](/basic/world/tutorials/a-mood-for-each-zone) | A swamp with a mood of its own and a cave in it with no sun | A look for each zone, event looks that win, the Debugger's *Look at* |

You can follow them in the [template project](/general/template). Everything is in the editor and nothing needs code.

::: info Status of the tutorials
They are written from the fields of the editors. They are being checked step by step in the editor, one at a time. The status of each is at the top of its page.
:::
