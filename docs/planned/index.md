# Planned features

These pages describe parts of ChronicleNode that are **not built yet**. They are here so you can see where the toolkit is going and plan your game around it. They say what is intended, what the toolkit already has that the feature will build on, and what is still undecided.

::: warning Not available yet
Nothing on these pages can be used today. A page can change before its feature ships. When a feature is built, its page moves into the [Basic guide](/basic/) and the [Advanced section](/advanced/).
:::

| Feature | What it is | What exists today |
|---|---|---|
| [Flying](/planned/flying) | Entities that can fly because of what they are, an ability or an effect | Flying in a [weightless movement volume](/basic/world/movement-volumes), and the claims that anything can add |
| [The World Builder](/planned/world-builder) | What is left of it: local events (a switch wired to a door, drawn in the 3D view), marks on the ground, local weather, streaming of what stands on the ground | The terrain tool, placement, [zones](/basic/world/zones), [spawners](/basic/world/spawners), [ground weather](/basic/world/ground-weather) and [terrain streaming](/basic/world/terrain-streaming) are built |
| [Localization](/planned/localization) | What is left of the localization tool: more forms for money and a few small leftovers |
| [Chat box, messages and the combat log](/planned/chat-box) | One message manager with channels; the chat box, toasts and labels are viewers; the combat log becomes a tab; detailed, optionally kept combat logs; typing and developer commands | A [combat log](/basic/user-interface/hud) in the HUD and short [messages](/basic/user-interface/messages-and-popups) that fade |
| [Zones](/planned/zones) | What is left of the [zones](/basic/world/zones): a look, weather and sound of their own with a blend at the border, discovered zones, saving, a zone map | [World configs](/basic/world/world-configs), default weather, the zones that are built |
| [Spawners](/planned/spawners) | What is left of the [spawners](/basic/world/spawners): faction and behaviour settings of the spawner itself | The three modes, the paint tools, the events, conditions and trigger, and the hours, weather and event conditions of a spawner |
| [The NPC behaviour tool](/planned/npc-behaviour-tool) | See and edit what an NPC does in the 3D view: its patrol paths and its behaviour | [Behavior scripts](/basic/behaviors/behavior-scripts) with a list of waypoints |
