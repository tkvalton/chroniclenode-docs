# ProficienciesEditor

**Inherits:** [ResourceEditor](/advanced/editor/base/resource-editor) < [Control](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_control.html)

Proficiencies Editor: the skills a player gets better at by use or training (swords, heavy armor, lockpicking). The name, description, icon and color are the base editor's. The left column holds the levels, what the skill is for, how it grows and what it gates; the right column holds the stat effects of the skill (the level is their points). See docs/systems/entity-stats.md, sections 29 and 31.
