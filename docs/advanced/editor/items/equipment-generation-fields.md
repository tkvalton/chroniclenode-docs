# EquipmentGenerationFields

**Inherits:** [VBoxContainer](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_vboxcontainer.html)

The "Generation" section of the equipment editor: what an item rolls when it is generated (loot, rewards, vendors). Bonus stats and effects can be randomized: affixes are picked from a pool, how many by the quality of the item, and the budget of the item says how much they give. What is written on the item counts toward the budget. A budget check shows what the written part spends against what an item level and quality give. See docs/systems/ability-ranks-and-item-generation.md, section 7.
