# KillFalloffEditor

**Inherits:** [VBoxContainer](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_vboxcontainer.html)

The editor of "Kill Experience Falloff" in the Gameplay Config (Leveling): an NPC under the level of the party gives less experience. A switch, the levels that cost nothing, and the curve: the number of levels under the party becomes the percent of the experience that is lost. A small table shows the result for a few levels. It writes straight to the config and tells the Gameplay Config editor to save.
