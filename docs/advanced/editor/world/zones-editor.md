# ZonesEditor

**Inherits:** [ResourceEditor](/advanced/editor/base/resource-editor) < [Control](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_control.html)

The Zones editor (the ZoneDefinition database): the named places of the world. A zone has a name, a colour, a parent (a sub-zone is a part of its parent) and the line of its banner. Where a zone is, is painted on the terrain (the Zones tool) or given by a zone volume. See docs: World &gt; Zones.
