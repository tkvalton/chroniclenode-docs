# AlbumTrackForm

**Inherits:** [ConfigForm](/advanced/editor/world/config-form) < [VBoxContainer](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_vboxcontainer.html)

The form of one track of a Music Album (an AlbumTrack): the file (chosen in the track window of the audio library), its weight and its volume. A built scene (album_track_form.tscn) of field components; it is the form of the items of the Tracks list of the Music Albums tab (Audio &gt; Music Albums).
